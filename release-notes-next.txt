<u><b>User Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new layout on computers: your profile and the sections down the left, the one you pick on the right, with a search</li>
<li>On phones: one see-through bar with the search built in, your name and badge at the top (tap it for General), and Log Out beside it</li>
<li>Tap your badge and it grows to full size with its colours under it, so you see each colour on it as you pick</li>
<li>Tap a setting's name to see what it does. Bigger switches, and simpler name and number editing</li>
<li>Notifications no longer has a second search box of its own. The settings search finds every notification, and picking one opens Notifications on just that one, with Show all to see the rest</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Projects</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Calmer scroll bars in the project's drop-down menus</li>
<li>Edit piece: double click a part in the CNC Cutlist, or right-click it there or in Nesting, to change it in its own pop-up. It looks like its row in the Cutlist List with every field ready to change, and saving it updates the part everywhere</li>
<li>The CNC Cutlist has a right-click menu: Edit piece, Show in the Production Cutlist, Change grain direction and Copy size</li>
<li>Right-clicking a row or card anywhere in the app lifts it a little and dims everything else while its menu is open. Clicking away from a right-click menu just closes it, without opening what you clicked on</li>
<li>Nesting's right-click menu can change a piece's grain direction. It only turns if the piece still fits its sheet that way, otherwise a yellow note says why</li>
<li>In the Cutlist List and the CNC Cutlist, linked drawer fronts sit together in one green box</li>
<li>Changing the part type of one linked drawer front changes the whole bank with it, so its fronts stay together</li>
<li>Cutlist Entry: a cabinet's Drilled dropdown is the same size as the other dropdowns</li>
<li>A door's hinge drawing in the Cutlist List now grows when you point at it, as it does in the Cutlist Entry, opening out like a menu. Click it and it grows into a big view in the middle of the screen, with the part's name at the top and each door's sizes along its sides</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Dashboard and Leads</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new <b>Users</b> filter beside Sort: tick the people whose projects or leads you want to see (or Unassigned). Works in the list and the columns, and it's remembered</li>
<li>A column's sort menu now says when the whole board's Sort is in charge, and what it's set to</li>
<li>When filtering by user, the filter button shows their badges</li>
<li>Inside a column's sub status, the back button sits in the title bar, where the folder icon is</li>
<li>Calmer scroll bars in the list view</li>
<li>Switching between the columns and the list glides the page back to the top first, instead of jumping</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Phones</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Swiping between Dashboard and Leads columns is smooth again, and a fast swipe always settles on a column. Every other swipe in the app is quicker to respond</li>
<li>Swiping sideways along a row of tabs or filters, or a wide table, scrolls it instead of opening a side menu (the Archive's tabs and months)</li>
<li>Tapping the page you're already on in the side menu closes the menu</li>
<li>With the keyboard up, tapping anywhere else just puts the keyboard away, without also opening or pressing what you tapped</li>
<li>Contacts' title bar, search and the top bar are see-through like the other pages, with your contacts scrolling behind them</li>
<li>User Settings' back button is round and green like the others, and Archive Project matches the other buttons</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Everywhere</b></u>
<ul>
<li>The Log Out pop-up's buttons match the rest of the app</li>
<li>Search only shows its loading wheel the first time. After that it updates quietly while your last results are already there</li>
<li>Every search box works the same way: the same X to clear it, in the same place, and the same look while you're using it. On phones, Contacts' search opens and closes like the Dashboard's</li>
<li>Notifications: a select button to pick several at once, then mark them read or unread, or clear them</li>

On phones its boxes are rounded too, and you can swipe it down to close it</li>
