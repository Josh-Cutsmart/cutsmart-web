<u><b>Quote & Specifications</b></u>

<li>Added a <b>Reopen Edit</b> button once a version has been sent, including while viewing that exact sent/accepted version (it used to only show while looking at something else)</li>

<li>Fixed occasional lag when scrolling with a mouse wheel</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Design Tab</b></u>
<ul>

  <li>For the whole-project view: every room, collapsed by default and expandable, plus a summary reconciling Quote Extras, Custom Pricing and any discount down to the Project Total</li>
  <li>A Print button that matches what's shown on screen</li>
  </ul>
</li>

</ul>

<u><b>Mobile</b></u>

</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
