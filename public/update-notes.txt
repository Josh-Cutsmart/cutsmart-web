Version: v0.4.6

whatsNew: [
<u><b>Quote & Specifications</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New: template authors can mark Specifications cells "Confirmable" in the Company Settings builder, making them eligible for the "Mark for Confirmation" button in a live project — marking it in the template doesn't automatically turn it into an active question, staff still choose which ones to activate per project</li>
<li>Duplicating a row (the + button) now carries over its Confirmable eligibility/active state to the new row</li>
<li>"Mark for Confirmation" moved out of the toolbar into a floating button that appears next to whatever's selected (matching "Import from Quote"'s own style), instead of a fixed toolbar button — centers across a multi-cell selection and smoothly slides between rows instead of jumping, and now matches the green "Submitted Version" colour</li>
<li>"Import from Quote" now only appears while its linked zone is selected, pops in/out instead of always floating, sits outside the zone so it never covers content, and flashes green to confirm a successful import</li>
<li>Importing from Quote now also brings over bold/underline formatting, not just plain text</li>
<li>Importing from Quote now asks for confirmation first if the zone's text was edited by hand since the last import, instead of silently overwriting it</li>
<li>Fixed: a new project (or one reset from the template) could start with a cell already showing as "Pending" if it had been marked Confirmable in the template before — it now always starts blank until staff actively mark it in that project</li>
<li>Right-clicking a cell no longer does anything in a live project's Quote/Specifications windows — group management is a template-only action now</li>
<li>The Sections (Specifications) and Quote Extras sidebars now list groups in the same top-to-bottom order they appear on the sheet, instead of creation order — categorised groups keep that same order within their own category too</li>
<li>The floating bottom bar's buttons now pop in instead of fading in as they appear, matching the Import/Mark for Confirmation buttons</li>
<li>Fixed a few small animation glitches on the floating buttons (one sitting too low when it first appeared, a pop-in getting clipped by its own container, a brief flash right after a button popped in)</li>
<li>The <b>Client Portal</b> link now opens in a normal new tab instead of a cramped popup window</li>
<li>Staff previewing their own project's Client Portal link now see a small back/exit button to return to CutSmart — never shown to actual clients viewing their own link</li>
<li>Mobile: the bottom action strip (Save/Reset/Download/Client Portal/etc.) is no longer a row of cramped icon-only buttons — it's now a single blue <b>Actions</b> button that opens a small floating card listing every action as icon+text rows; drag it down or tap outside to dismiss</li>
<li>Mobile: holding down an unsent/unsubmitted row in Version History now opens the same delete confirmation desktop gets from hovering the row's delete icon (a normal tap still opens that version)</li>
<li>Mobile: holding down on a cell marked Confirmable now opens a small menu to mark it — or a whole highlighted group of cells — for confirmation, instead of needing the floating button; a normal tap still just selects the cell</li>
<li>Mobile: added an explicit <b>Back</b> button to the top of the Version History panel instead of relying only on the swipe gesture</li>
<li>Mobile: fixed the Accepted/Pending status badge getting squeezed out of sight in the title bar on narrow screens — it now shows in its own band just below the title</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Design Tab</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Fixed printing the Cost by Room breakdown sometimes coming out at the wrong size/scale, and on mobile leaving you stranded on a blank print tab — it now prints in place instead of opening a separate tab/window</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Mobile</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Fixed the browser's address bar/toolbar on iPhone unexpectedly sliding up and down on pages whose content doesn't fill the screen</li>
<li>The pull-down-to-switch-dashboard gesture now also works correctly from the Initial Measure, Production Cutlist, Nesting and CNC full-screen views (previously only worked reliably elsewhere)</li>
<li>Fixed pinch-zooming the Quote/Specifications sheet preview sometimes also triggering the pull-down or side-nav-swipe gesture underneath it</li>
<li>Fixed a rare case where an interrupted swipe (e.g. an incoming call mid-gesture) could leave a nav drawer, notification panel, or the new Actions sheet stuck half-open until the page was reloaded</li>
</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Popups and confirmation dialogs across the app (New Project, Log out, Leads, Recently Deleted, User Settings, Company Settings, Changelog, and most popups inside a project) now visually shrink back into the button that opened them when closed, with a small bounce, instead of just fading out in place</li>
<li>Fixed click-and-drag text selection being broken app-wide — dragging to select text now works normally again, while dragging cards/scrollbar handles/resize handles still correctly avoids selecting text underneath</li>
<li>Fixed clicking inside a dialog opened from the account/settings panel (e.g. the Log out confirmation) also closing the settings panel behind it</li>
<li>Fixed being logged into CutSmart in two browser tabs occasionally causing one tab to get silently logged out when the other one reloaded</li>
<li>The Quote tab's header icon changed from a quote-mark icon to a dollar-sign icon, matching its sidebar nav icon</li>
