<u><b>Calendar</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New: a <b>Calendar</b> tab — a shared company calendar with Month, Week, Day and List views</li>
<li>Click a day or time to add an event with a title, category, all-day or start/end times, location, notes and an optional linked project</li>
<li>Drag events to move them, and drag the bottom edge of an event in Week/Day view to change its length</li>
<li>Events that run over several days show as one continuous bar, and keep their full length while you drag them</li>
<li>Every day in Month view is the same height and fills with as many events as fit, with a <b>+count</b> pill next to the date for the rest</li>
<li>Events are listed top to bottom in the same order as the categories in Company Settings</li>
<li>Clicking an event opens a view of just the event — coloured in its category — with an <b>Edit</b> button for people who can change it</li>
<li>Each event shows who added it, with their emblem, in the bottom bar</li>
<li>Show or hide categories from the filter list</li>
<li>Days that aren't workdays are shown crossed out</li>
<li>Desktop: Previous and Next slide to the next month (up/down) or the next week, day or list (left/right)</li>
<li>Mobile: the calendar runs edge to edge in every view</li>
<li>Mobile: swipe up and down to move between months — it locks onto each month, the next one is already loaded, and the greyed days either side fade over instead of showing twice</li>
<li>Mobile: swiping in the calendar no longer opens the pull-down menu or the side drawers</li>
<li>Mobile: the event pop-up no longer has a Close button, since the X is in the top corner</li>
</ul>
