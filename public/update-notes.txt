* Removed the card/border wrapper around sheet previews on each board page — they now sit directly on the page background, with the board type name no longer repeated per-board (it's already shown once at the top)
* Sheets within a board stay in a single column (was briefly showing as rows)
* Visibility is now a true slide-up panel: drag up from the bottom bar (or tap it) to open, drag down (or tap) the panel's own header to close, with a drag handle, an expanding search box, and a "Show All" button that fades in once open; the panel now reaches the very top and bottom of the screen
* Visibility button fills the full width and edges of its bottom bar
* Fixed a white flash during the board-type swipe transition
* Top bar, board-type bar, and the Visibility bar are now plain white on mobile
* Added a thin glass-style scrollbar that stays put while swiping between board types instead of sliding along with the page

<u><b>CNC Cutlist (Mobile)</b></u>
* Fixed the prev/next board-type arrows not actually changing board (they updated the title but never scrolled)
* Board-type bar redesigned to match Nesting's: fixed position, bigger centred title with a count pill, black chevrons, white background
* Mobile Visibility rebuilt to match Nesting's new slide-up panel exactly (desktop's own sidebar panel is unchanged)
* Row list simplified to Part Type, Part Name, Size (showing whichever of Height/Width/Depth are filled in), and Quantity — tap a row to see everything else (Room, Height/Width/Depth, Quantity, Clashing, Information) in a popup
* That popup now sizes itself to its content instead of filling the screen, with black text and each group of fields under its own shaded label bar
* Added the same glass-style scrollbar as Nesting

<u><b>Top Tab Bar</b></u>
