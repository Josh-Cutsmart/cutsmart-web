whatsNew: [
<u><b>Notifications</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new <b>Notifications</b> panel that slides in from the right on a computer</li>
<li>The bell shows how many you haven't read</li>
<li>Each kind of notification has its own icon</li>
<li>Right-click (or press and hold) a notification to mark it read, clear it, or be <b>reminded later</b></li>
<li>New ones pop up in the corner for a few seconds on a computer</li>
<li>Clearer messages — an accepted quote shows its price</li>
<li>Shows the last 2 weeks, with <b>Load more</b> for older ones</li>
<li>Projects you created now notify you by default</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Calendar</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Set your own reminders for each calendar category, in <b>User Settings › Notifications</b></li>
<li>Events show who added and last changed them</li>
<li>New <b>…</b> menu on an event: <b>Duplicate</b> or <b>Delete</b></li>
<li>New events need a title and a category</li>
<li>Search also finds events by location, notes, project and category</li>
<li>Phones: swipe between days and weeks</li>
<li>Show or hide non-work days just for you, in <b>User Settings</b></li>
<li>A tidier toolbar on computers and phones</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Projects</b></u>
<ul>
<li>The selected tab sits in a glass pill that slides between tabs</li>
<li>Notes: click to type, click away to save</li>
<li>Design has its own <b>Notes</b> now</li>
<li>Client Details: the edit pencil shows when you point at it, and <b>Create Contact</b> sits beside the client's name</li>
<li>Choosing the creator or assigned user shows their badge, with a search</li>
<li><b>Reopen for edit</b> takes the quote or specifications back from the client</li>
<li>A quote can't be reopened once it's been accepted</li>
<li>Projects open faster from the top bar</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Dashboard &amp; Leads</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Cards glide into place when you drop them</li>
<li>Tidier column buttons: collapse on the left, sort on the right</li>
<li>Leads column names stay easy to read on dark colours</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Company Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Inviting someone also gives them a one-person join code</li>
<li>Item categories: drag items to put them in order</li>
<li>Deleting a category or an item asks first</li>
<li>A new logo shows on quotes and specifications you've already sent</li>
</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
<ul>
<li>One background colour on every page</li>
<li>Phones: bigger buttons and menus, and menus slide over the page</li>
<li>Text boxes show a thin blue outline while you type</li>
<li>Company Settings is now below Archive in the sidebar</li>
<li>Faster — CutSmart's server is now in Sydney</li>
</ul>
]
