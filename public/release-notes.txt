whatsNew: [
<u><b>Dashboard</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Right-click a project — its card, or its tab up top; on a phone or tablet, the <b>⋯</b> on its card — for <b>Open</b> (or straight to Design or Production), <b>Move</b>, <b>Change Assigned</b>, <b>Edit Tags</b> and <b>Archive</b></li>
<li>Right-click a column and choose <b>Automate</b>: <b>Notify</b> people or roles when a project enters it, leaves it or has been in it a while — or <b>Move</b> it on, <b>Assign</b> it or <b>Tag</b> it</li>
<li>Add <b>Only if…</b> to an automation — who it's assigned to, where it came from, its tags or its name — and it waits until that's true</li>
<li>The Automate pop-up takes its column's colour, as the calendar's pop-ups take their category's</li>
<li>A timed move can count from when each project moved in, so the ones already there count too</li>
<li>Whoever moves a card is told what their move set off</li>
<li>A status with sub-statuses shows the sub-status in a pill of its own — each half opens its own menu</li>
<li>The list's status menu slides through to a status's sub-statuses</li>
<li>Each board has its name over it, with a back arrow inside a sub-board</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Small Jobs</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new page for quick jobs and small fixes — "Replace toekick", "Change hinges" — without a whole project</li>
<li>Each has its client, notes and images, and its own statuses: Open and Closed to start with, yours in Company Settings › <b>Small Jobs</b></li>
<li>Search and filter them, and turn one into a full project when it grows</li>
<li>Kept off the Dashboard and out of Reports</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Reports</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new page: leads converted, quote win rate, average job value and days to complete — each against the stretch before</li>
<li>Quotes sent against won by week or month, the days spent in each stage, the pipeline now, each salesperson's figures and where your leads came from</li>
<li>Choose which staff and projects your reports count, and <b>Reset stats</b> to start counting afresh</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Leads</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Create Project</b> fills the new project in from the lead — its name, the client's details, notes, photos, who it's assigned to and their other answers — and files the lead as converted</li>
<li>Automations work here too — including moving a lead onto a Dashboard column, which makes it into a project there</li>
<li>Copy buttons beside a lead's phone number and email</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Projects</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Schedule</b>: the project's calendar events along a timeline, spaced by date, with today and what's next — click one to open it in the Calendar</li>
<li>A project made from a lead has a <b>Lead</b> button on its General tab, to see the lead it came from</li>
<li>Copy buttons beside the client's phone, email and address</li>
<li>The client's contact category in a pill beside their name — change it there and it's changed everywhere</li>
<li>Every pop-up has the same header, and fills the screen on a phone</li>
<li>In dark mode a project's cards are see-through dark glass, as Reports' are — and Production's Notes card matches the others</li>
<li>Cutlist Entry's column headings are easy to read in dark mode, held at the top too</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Production</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Custom shapes</b>: set a part type to Custom in Company Settings and its pieces are drawn — an L corner, a U, a notch, an angled corner, or any shape you draw line by line (snapping to 45° and 90°). Tap any size to change it, any corner to round it, any edge to band it</li>
<li>A shaped piece's cut size, banding and drawing go on its cutlist row, and its banding counts in the board's edge tape</li>
<li>Round a corner into a curve by dragging its handle, and a shape can't be drawn crossing itself</li>
<li>Shaped pieces have a <b>Custom</b> table of their own — open one for a large drawing, <b>Edit shape</b> and <b>Download DXF</b> for the CNC</li>
<li>Shaped pieces show as drawings in the CNC Cutlist, on the Nesting sheets, on labels and in the printed cutlist and Excel — with lacquer worked out from their true area</li>
<li><b>Custom cabinets</b>: set "What is it?" to a cabinet, give it a height and shelves, and its backs, ends, top, bottom and shelves are worked out at the board's thickness — turn it round in 3D, and its parts go through the CNC Cutlist, Nesting, labels, edge tape and sheet counts</li>
<li>Initial Measure takes shaped pieces and custom cabinets too</li>
<li>A room with nothing in its cutlist yet shows <b>Add your first piece</b> — press it and pick the part type (Initial Measure and Production)</li>
<li>Cutlist Entry's column headings only show while there are rows being entered</li>
<li><b>Order</b> redesigned: the order at a glance up top, Drawers, Hinges and Other side by side, then Boards and Misc</li>
<li>Drag hinges and other parts in from the <b>Hardware Library</b>, or click one to add it</li>
<li>Two people in the Order see each other's changes as they're made</li>
<li>Empty Hinges and Other show a dashed box to add the first one</li>
<li><b>Copy for cart</b>: the Order's hinges and other parts, a line each, ready to paste into your supplier's quick order — Blum's <b>Add items in bulk</b></li>
<li>Copy a drawer's, hinge's or part's ID number straight from the Order</li>
<li>A drawer's runner length is picked from the lengths its height comes in</li>
<li><b>Labels</b>: a label for every piece, with a QR code — scan it with a phone to see the part, its room and its project</li>
<li><b>Print</b>: the cutlist laid out like the old spreadsheet, a title sheet, and several prints together as one Project Folder</li>
<li>Cutlist: <b>Select</b> parts to change their board or edging all at once, or delete them</li>
<li>Cutlist: <b>Save &amp; Back</b> checks for a missing board, a zero quantity or a grain clash first</li>
<li>Doors show a drawing of the door with its hinges — left, right, a mirrored pair or along the top</li>
<li>Drawer heights stack in the list, beside their depths</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Contacts</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A quiet <b>Timeline</b> button on a contact: every project, quote and lead they've had, along one line</li>
<li>Contacts that look like the same person are flagged at the top — review them side by side and merge, or mark them as not duplicates</li>
<li>Copy buttons beside phone, email and address</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Calendar</b></u>
<ul>
<li>NZ public holidays, worked out for every year — pick your region in Company Settings › <b>Calendar</b> for its anniversary day</li>
<li>Today is outlined in blue</li>
<li>Previous and Next slide the view along</li>
<li><b>Schedule a project</b>: a panel of your projects and small jobs — the ones with nothing booked yet first — to drag onto a day, or an hour, to book it in — or onto an event to link it to that one (unlinked events that sound like it lift and glow while you drag)</li>
<li>Typing a new event's title suggests the project it's about (just while you first type it), and clicking <b>Project</b> shows the closest straight away, with <b>Show all</b> for the rest</li>
<li>The event pop-up: the times sit under the title, date and time wheels on phones, and the category list opens downwards</li>
<li>The time wheel goes round and round, to any minute</li>
<li>The event pop-up takes on more of its category's colour</li>
<li>The date wheel shows the weekday, and its days and months scroll on into the month (and year) either side</li>
<li>Add staff shows each person's coloured initials</li>
<li>The month view fits more events in each day</li>
<li>Scroll through months as fast as you like — the two months either side are always ready, with their events, so it doesn't bounce back</li>
<li>Switching to Day, Week, Month or Year starts on today — not wherever you'd scrolled to</li>
<li>Open a day from the month, week or year and a <b>‹</b> takes you back to it — at the bottom left on a phone, sliding in to the left of Today on a computer</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Company Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Staff &amp; Permissions: an <b>Audit log</b> of every role and permission change — who made it and when</li>

<li>Backup &amp; Output: <b>Full data export</b> — everything your company has, in one file to keep</li>

<li>Small Jobs: their own statuses</li>
<li>Search finds every card</li>
