whatsNew: [
<u><b>Log In &amp; Sign Up</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new log in page: just a log in box — on a computer, with CutSmart's features sliding by beside it; on a phone, full screen</li>
<li>Every step slides on to the next: log in or create an account, verify your email, then create or join a company</li>
<li>After you log in, <b>create a company</b> or <b>join one with its code</b> — if you're already in a company, it opens straight away</li>
<li>Company invites you've been sent show up after you log in, to accept or decline</li>
<li>After creating an account you can set a 4-digit <b>PIN</b> (or skip it) — CutSmart then stays signed in on that device and asks for your PIN when you come back to it</li>
<li>CutSmart only keeps you signed in if you tick <b>Remember me</b> (or have a PIN)</li>
<li>Creating a company: its name and code no longer fill themselves in</li>
</ul>

<u><b>CutSmart Preview</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New: <b>Try the CutSmart Preview</b> on the log in page — a demo company with projects, leads, a calendar, rooms, items and cutlists already filled in, to look around and edit without an account</li>
<li>Nothing in the Preview is saved: close it and it starts fresh next time. Printing, PDFs and exports are turned off</li>
<li>Its dates always fall in the current month, and its Changelog is CutSmart's real one</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Company Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Your company's name, ID and plan are now in the <b>Company</b> tab (they were at the bottom of the sidebar)</li>
<li>New <b>Join key</b> button: shows your company's master code, with <b>Change</b> — changing it doesn't remove anyone already in the company</li>
<li><b>Temporary codes</b>: make a code for one person to join with, and revoke it at any time. Revoking one that's been used first asks what to do with that person's work, the same as removing a staff member — so they can't just rejoin with the master code</li>
<li>Each staff member shows the join code they used</li>
<li>New <b>Invited</b> list under Staff: everyone you've invited who hasn't joined yet, with <b>Cancel invite</b></li>
</ul>

<u><b>Dashboard</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A cleaner look: the page, its toolbar, the project list and the top bar are all one flat colour, in light and dark mode, with no lines between them</li>
<li>The lines between projects fade out at each end, and so does the highlight on the one you're pointing at (in dark mode it's darker, not lighter)</li>
<li>The search bar and every toolbar button have fully rounded ends, and line up with the stat cards and the list</li>
<li>The stat cards' shadows are no longer cut off</li>
<li>Switching between list and board: the buttons that belong to each pop in and out</li>
<li>Mobile: search is a round button that opens into the full search bar, and Sort is a round button too</li>
<li>Dark mode: the search bar matches the buttons beside it</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Kanban Boards</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Dashboard and Leads: as the board scrolls into place, its columns grow smoothly with it, their bottoms staying just above the bottom of the screen</li>
<li>Scrolled all the way, the columns stop as far below the top bar as they sit above the bottom of the screen, with the toolbar tucked away under the top bar</li>
<li>Collapsing a column slides it into its narrow strip, its button riding across to where the sort button was and flipping round on the way — expanding slides it back out</li>
<li>Bigger sort, count and collapse buttons on each column</li>
<li>Searching slides the board to the first column with a match — press <b>Enter</b> to go on to the next — and the card glows so it's easy to spot</li>
<li>The columns' shadows are no longer cut off along their tops</li>
</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Top bar: each tab is as wide as its project's name, with fully rounded ends</li>
<li>Top bar: see-through at the top of a page — its glass fades in once the page scrolls under it</li>
<li>Pointing at a tab gives it a faint blue outline (which turns red over its X), and the project you're in keeps its outline</li>
<li>Closing a tab pops it away, and the tabs after it slide over</li>
<li>Dragging a tab: it follows you straight away, the others move aside as soon as it passes them, and it slides into its place when you let go</li>
<li>The sidebar's page names are no longer bold</li>
<li>Mobile: the pull-down menu matches the Dashboard's colours</li>
<li>The What's New page is calmer — its lists of changes stay put instead of floating</li>
</ul>
]
