whatsNew: [
<u><b>Items</b></u>
<ul>
<li>The item library groups each category's items by sub-category, under bars in their own colours</li>
<li>Click a category or sub-category to fold it away — it stays the way you left it</li>
<li>Searching opens the groups with a match</li>
<li>Type a quantity on an item before you click or drag it in — an <b>Added</b> pill shows it went in</li>
<li>Each room is a column like the Dashboard's: collapse it, see its count, and use its <b>…</b> menu to Rename, Duplicate, Clear or Delete</li>
<li>Drag a room by its header to put your rooms in order</li>
<li>A room's items sit under coloured bars like the library, with − and + when you point at them</li>
<li>Removing an item (or taking it down from 1) asks first</li>
<li>The Summary floats on the right, with <b>Add Room</b> under it</li>
<li>A tidier <b>Add Item</b> pop-up with its own search</li>
<li>Phones: rooms are cards down the page (hold a card's header to move it), and Add Item fills the screen</li>
<li>Two items with the same name no longer cause errors</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Projects</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New <b>Schedule</b> button in Design: the Calendar's events for the project, in a list</li>
<li>New <b>Specifications</b> button in Production: the specifications the client confirmed — or the latest, marked <b>Unconfirmed</b></li>
<li>Adding a room offers your company's room names, and you can still type your own</li>
<li>The Product box in Design widens to fit long product names</li>
<li><b>Specifications</b> and <b>Upgrades</b> have swapped places in Design</li>
<li>Phones: Notes bring the keyboard up on the first tap</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Production</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New <b>Import</b>: bring in the Excel cutlist (or an older cutlist sheet), choose which parts and rooms, then add to or replace what's there</li>
<li>Rooms can have their own hardware — the Hardware box lists Global, then each room</li>
<li>Changing a brand or drawer type shows the drawers it affects first, with quick fixes</li>
<li>Drawer heights show their size in mm</li>
<li>Notes open in a drawer on the right that stays open while you work</li>
<li><b>Contractors</b> is now a menu — pick one to open their notes</li>
<li>Changing a board in Board Settings asks whether to update the cutlist rows using it</li>
<li>Order groups drawers by brand and drawer type when rooms differ</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Calendar</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Add staff to an event — they get its reminders</li>
<li>Linking a project adds its creator and assigned person</li>
<li>Search stays open while there's something in it</li>
<li>Phones: the Categories button's icon no longer gets squashed</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Company Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New <b>Rooms</b> list (in Sales and Production): the room names offered when adding a room</li>
<li>Item categories: a <b>Custom</b> price — staff enter it each time it's added, with your markup on top</li>
<li>The sidebar's resize bar no longer shows over Item Categories and the layout builders</li>
</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New <b>Downloads</b> in the sidebar, with the Production Cutlist for Excel</li>
<li>Menus open upwards when there's no room below them</li>
<li>User Settings: Non-Work Days can be Company, Show or Hide</li>
<li>Contacts: badges show letters only</li>
<li>Dashboard: the faint line near the bottom of the columns is gone</li>
<li>A rounded glass formatting toolbar in Notes</li>
</ul>
]
