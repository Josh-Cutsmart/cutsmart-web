whatsNew: [
<u><b>Project Drawings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A new <b>Drawing</b> in Production, under Planning: the kitchen in 3D, saved with the project</li>
<li>The cutlist's pieces wait in a library down the left, each at its board's thickness — drag them onto the drawing</li>
<li>Fronts dropped on the floor start a cabinet, sized from the fronts and your gap allowances. A cabinet from the cutlist comes in at its own size, drawn as its pieces</li>
<li>Pieces snap together: overheads match the height of what's beside them, and base and overhead cabinets sit at the front of a panel, with room left for their fronts</li>
<li><b>Move</b>, <b>Rotate</b>, <b>Extrude</b> and <b>Measure</b> tools — type a distance or an angle straight away, as it's already selected for you</li>
<li><b>Measure</b> between points, edges or faces, and read the angle at a corner</li>
<li>Each piece has corner points at its top and bottom: drag one onto another piece's and the two join exactly</li>
<li>Options for each piece: <b>Wall or Base</b>, <b>Benchtop</b>, <b>Kick</b>, <b>Fronts</b>, <b>Show in drawing</b> and <b>Lock position</b></li>
<li>One benchtop over each run, filled in round its corners, with overhangs you can type and faces you can pull out</li>
<li>Turn a run round a corner at any angle, swing a panel across as a face panel, add kick boards, and leave a gap for a dishwasher or fridge</li>
<li>A view cube, and <b>Ctrl+Z</b> (⌘Z) to step back</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Automations</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Email</b> as a new automation: send to staff, typed addresses, or whoever's <b>Assigned</b> or the <b>Creator</b></li>
<li>Write the whole email yourself, subject and body, with placeholders for the project, the client and more</li>
<li>Send it straight away, or after a wait you choose</li>
<li>Notify or email more than one person: "notify Anna &amp; Ben when a project moves to Production"</li>
<li><b>View All</b> shows every automation in the company, in Company Settings › <b>Automations</b></li>
<li>A <b>Log</b> there shows each time an automation ran, when, and how it went</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Dashboard</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Right-click a project — its card, or its tab up top; on a phone or tablet, the <b>⋯</b> on its card — to open it, <b>Move</b> it, <b>Change Assigned</b>, <b>Edit Tags</b>, <b>Archive</b> it or <b>View Access</b>, which shows its Project Permissions in a pop-up</li>
<li>Sort a column by <b>Longest in column</b> or <b>Newest in column</b>, on Leads too</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Production</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Custom shapes</b>: set a part type to Custom in Company Settings and its pieces are drawn — an L corner, a U, a notch, an angled corner, or any shape line by line. Type any size, and drag a corner into a curve</li>
<li><b>Custom cabinets</b>: give a piece a height and shelves, and its backs, ends, top, bottom and shelves are worked out. Base or Wall, with Full Top, fixed and adjustable shelves, rails, and doors edges taped on every part</li>
<li>Shapes and cabinets carry through the CNC Cutlist, Nesting, labels, print and Excel, with <b>Download DXF</b> for the CNC and <b>Export</b> as a PNG or PDF. Initial Measure takes them too</li>
<li><b>True-shape nesting</b> on a CNC: pieces fit into each other by their real outline — never more sheets than before</li>
<li><b>Nesting</b> redesigned on a computer: your boards on the left, their sheets in the middle and the one you pick open large. Leave out any piece, and see which sheet each one is on</li>
<li><b>Small Jobs have a cutlist and Boards</b>, the same as a project's Production</li>
<li><b>Copy for cart</b>: the Order's hinges and other parts, ready to paste into your supplier's quick order. Copy a single ID number from the Order too</li>
<li>Type a sum in a Height, Width or Depth box — 50-16, 600x2, 1200/3 — and it's worked out when you click away</li>
<li><b>Contractor icons</b>, set in Company Settings, on their notes button, menu and notes</li>
<li><b>Viewing</b> and <b>Editing</b> bubbles in the Cutlist, Order and Initial Measure show who else is there, and they pop away as people leave</li>
<li><b>Unlock Edit</b> requests can be answered from the notification: <b>Accept</b> or <b>Decline</b> right on it, or open it for a box with both</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Calendar</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Schedule a project</b>: drag a project or small job onto a day or an hour to book it in, or onto an event to link it</li>
<li>A new event's title suggests the project it's about</li>
<li>Date and time wheels that keep on turning, with the weekday shown</li>
<li>Months scroll as fast as you like, with no bouncing back</li>
<li>Open a day from the month, week or year and a <b>‹</b> takes you back to it</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Notifications</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Remind me in…</b> keeps a notification in your list, with a <b>Reminder in 2h</b> bubble (its <b>X</b> cancels it), then brings it back as new at that time</li>
<li>Opening Notifications clears the number on the bell, and they stay unread in the list</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Company Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Unlock Edit password</b>: choose what it starts with — the project name, the client's name, a word of your own or nothing — then your code. Spaces and capitals don't count</li>
<li><b>Access codes</b>: make a one-person code to join with, name it, see who used it and revoke it any time. <b>Add staff</b> gives each person one, along with the role they start in</li>
<li>Hardware drawers laid out afresh, with an ID number for each height, length and weight, and an <b>Ordering</b> tab for how the Order copies them for your supplier's cart</li>
</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Delete anything — a cutlist row, a board, a tag, an image, an event — and the yellow bar offers <b>Undo</b> for a few seconds</li>
<li>Lose signal and a small bar says so. Your changes are kept on the device and saved when you're back</li>
<li>The first project you open comes up quicker</li>
<li>A link that goes nowhere shows a proper page with the way back, and if something goes wrong the app offers <b>Try again</b></li>
</ul>

<u><b>Phones</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Swipe a pop-up down to close it</li>
<li>+ − × ÷ = over the number keypad while typing a size in the cutlist</li>
<li>Typing in a box brings it to the middle of the screen, above the keyboard</li>
</ul>
]
