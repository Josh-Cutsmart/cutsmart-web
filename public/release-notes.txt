whatsNew: [
<u><b>Leads</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A simpler page like the Dashboard: one bar on top with the same search, a status filter and sort menus, and rounded buttons</li>
<li>Stat cards for <b>Active</b>, <b>New</b>, <b>Converted</b> and <b>Unassigned</b></li>
<li>A list view laid out like the Dashboard's — and both pages stay in the view you picked</li>
<li>Build lead forms in Company Settings › <b>Leads</b> — as many as you like, with your own questions — and put them on your website</li>
<li>Your company has its own leads email address: forward an email to it and it comes in as a lead, photos and all</li>
<li>New leads come in as <b>New Lead · From Email</b> or <b>New Lead · From Form</b>, in your first status</li>
<li>Click a lead to edit any field, change its status, see its photos, and add notes and checklists beside it</li>
<li><b>+ Create Lead</b> floats in the bottom corner and opens a pop-up</li>
<li>Leads load faster</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Dashboard</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Completed</b> is rebuilt: every job moved into a completed status is stamped with its day and time, and stays — even if it's archived or deleted</li>
<li>See how many were completed by month, quarter, half year or year, with a chart of the periods before it to drag through</li>
<li>Bonus quarters (with the bonus permission): drag a job onto a quarter — its left half for the bonus, its right half for small jobs — and its status locks</li>
<li><b>Copy quarter</b> copies its jobs as tables, ready for an email or spreadsheet</li>
<li>Jobs with an accepted quote under your bonus minimum show <b>Below $…</b></li>
<li>Change a job's completed date from a small calendar, or drag it to the bin to take it off the list</li>
<li>A floating bar on desktop for opening sub-status boards</li>
<li>The status filter is a dropdown, and the Kanban gets a sort button</li>
<li>Empty columns no longer say "No Projects"</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Archive</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Redesigned as a filing cabinet: projects, leads, contacts and calendar events, filed by year and month</li>
<li>Filter by type, search, and fold away the years and months — it opens the way you left it</li>
<li>Deleting a project for good can email a copy to your team first: its client details, quote, specifications, cutlists and images</li>
<li>A deleted project's files and images are cleared away too</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Projects</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Client Details and their contact card stay the same — change one and the other (and their other projects) follow</li>
<li>Click the client's badge to open their contact card right there</li>
<li>Design: <b>Extra Charges</b> to switch on for each project, with a quantity</li>
<li>Design: prices show <b>incl GST</b> or <b>excl GST</b></li>
<li>Design: <b>Nesting</b> on each product in Sheets shows how the parts fit on the sheets</li>
<li>Design: a simpler Project Cost Breakdown, and room breakdowns use your item category pills</li>
<li>Quote window: <b>Accept</b> for the client, and Specifications: <b>Submit</b> for the client — marked "on behalf of" the client, with who did it</li>
<li>Images and Files: drag in to add, delete shows when something's selected, and their buttons show on hover</li>
<li>Locked jobs say why in a bar at the bottom when you try to change their status</li>
<li>The bell now fills in when you're subscribed</li>
<li>The scrollbar no longer jumps under the sticky bars</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Production</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Pop a contractor's notes out into a floating bubble: drag it anywhere, resize it, shrink it to a pill, and drag it back onto its button to put it away</li>
<li>Notes open where you left them for that project</li>
<li>Up to 5 contractors get their own buttons</li>
<li>Two people in the same cutlist (Production or Initial Measure) see each other's changes as they're made, and saves never undo someone else's</li>
<li>A cutlist shows who else is editing it</li>
<li>A cutlist with one room opens on that room</li>
<li>The Cutlist Entry sits beside the Rooms button, with <b>Add to Cutlist</b> at its bottom</li>
<li>The part type menu takes the part type's colour</li>
<li>The Excel cutlist: working dropdowns, tickboxes for yes/no, boards that link up, and rows coloured by part type</li>
<li>Specifications just say <b>Confirmed</b> or <b>Unconfirmed</b></li>
</ul>

<u><b>Contacts</b></u>
<ul>
<li>A <b>Share</b> button to send a contact's details through another app</li>
<li>The same search as the Dashboard, and rounded buttons</li>
<li>Desktop: a contact opens over the top bar</li>
<li>All your contacts show every time you open the page</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Calendar</b></u>
<ul>
<li>Ring the bell on an event to get its reminders — you don't need to be on it</li>
<li>A project's creator is only added to its events when no one's assigned</li>
<li>Reminders only come at the times you set — turning one on no longer sends ones already gone</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Company Settings</b></u>
<ul>
<li>New <b>Leads</b> tab: your lead forms and your leads email address</li>
<li>Sales: Products is a table — sheet sizes with their own prices and margins, edge tape and lacquer, and row colours</li>
<li>Sales: <b>Extra Charges</b>, each one on every quote by default or not</li>
<li>Sales: include GST on quotes, at your rate</li>
<li>Dashboard: your bonus period, when it's paid, and the minimum quote for it</li>
<li>Statuses are numbered — the first is where new projects and leads start</li>
<li>Comes back to the tab you were on after a refresh</li>
</ul>

<u><b>App-Wide</b></u>
<ul>
<li><b>Forgot password?</b> on the log in screen emails you a link to set a new one</li>
<li>A glassier look for buttons, pills, stat cards and rows</li>
<li>Status dropdowns are simpler, with just a coloured dot</li>
<li>Pop-ups all share the same clear X</li>
<li>A project's creator only gets its notifications when they've rung its bell</li>
<li>Notifications that came in while your phone or computer was off arrive as one group</li>
<li>What's New no longer shows on every visit after an update</li>
</ul>

<u><b>Phones</b></u>
<ul>

</ul>
